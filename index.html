Command Terminal

Immutable operational lifecycle.

Complete control over the trading mandate via numeric identity authorization. Every command records idempotent intents without racing physical executions.

/run <id>

Starts or resumes an eligible trading bot after strict policy and balance reconciliation.

/pause <id>

Blocks entries, cancels pending buys, reconciles fills, and actively maintains protective exits.

/close <id>

Pauses entries, resolves pending buys, and immediately liquidates owned positions.

/halt

Owner-enforced account block. Completely fences new exposure while keeping existing limits alive.

/resume_account

Removes account halt. Requires fresh reconciliation and unblocked epoch execution.

/detail <id>

Displays reconciled positions, performance, risk baselines, and current admission reasons.
